Stack Tower – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Stack Tower ("the app") is a casual stacking game published by Xaplu. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

Your best score is saved on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your Google account backup so it is restored on a new device. This backup is handled by Google, and we cannot access it.

Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app uses Google Play Games Services to show leaderboards. Your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar) and the scores you submit are processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Your score and Play Games profile name may be visible to other players on the leaderboard, according to your Play Games settings. We do not receive your email address or any other Google account details.

Delete your data

Stack Tower does not keep any of your data on our own servers. To delete the data linked to the game:

  1. Leaderboard scores and Play Games data (player ID, gamer name, avatar, submitted scores): open your Play Games profile (or the Play Games app ▸ Profile ▸ Settings), find Stack Tower and choose Delete Play Games data, or delete your whole Play Games profile. Google deletes this data under its own retention policy.
  2. Your best score on the device: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Stack Tower ▸ Storage ▸ Clear storage. If it was included in your Google backup, you can remove the backup in your Google account's backup settings.

Nothing is kept by us after you do this. If you need help, email akaashhazarika@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
